# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in this
project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background or
identity.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insulting comments, and personal or
political attacks.

## Scope of the project

Persona is intended for legitimate multi-account management, testing and privacy
research. Contributions whose only purpose is to enable fraud, abuse, or illegal
activity are out of scope and will be declined.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards and may remove, edit, or
reject contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
